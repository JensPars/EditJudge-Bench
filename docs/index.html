Overview

Can VLM judges actually see the edits they score?

EditJudge-Bench is 1,382 before/after pairs rendered in Blender, with exact ground truth across nine edit types. It tests whether VLM judges can verify that an edit was done correctly. Across 26 judge setups, even strong judges fall short (VIEScore + GPT-4o: 69.0% AUROC), spatial edits stay near chance, and rankings still track real-image human preference (ρ up to 0.93).

The benchmark

Edits

Every edit is a deterministic change to a known Blender scene parameter, so its magnitude and direction are known exactly — and everything outside the edit is pixel-identical.
